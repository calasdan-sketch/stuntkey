StuntKey vs 1Password, Doppler, Infisical, dotenvx

These are good tools. They overlap with StuntKey, but they are made for different people. Below we say only what we checked, with a link. Anything else says “check their site”, because their products change.

1Password

  • Its Claude plugin needs a paid 1Password subscription, the 1Password desktop app, and Claude Code 2.1.273 or newer.
  • Its mounted .env file and its MCP connection work on Mac and Linux, not on Windows (as of November 2026).
  • The agent sees the names of your secrets only.
  • Everything else (prices, plans, what it does for a team): check their site.

Infisical Agent Vault

  • Agent Vault is an open-source credential proxy and vault for agents.
  • Everything else (setup, hosting, platforms): check their site.

Doppler

  • Doppler for agents gives scoped, read-only service tokens that expire, and doppler run puts secrets into a program's environment.
  • Everything else (prices, limits, how it fits your tools): check their site.

dotenvx

  • We have not checked what dotenvx does for AI agents, so we will not guess: check their site.

Where they are better

If you work with a team, need to share secrets between people, sync them between computers, keep audit records for a company, or run in the cloud, the tools above are made for that and StuntKey is not. StuntKey keeps your keys on one computer, for one person.

Where StuntKey fits

  • Free and open source, MIT licence. Python standard library only.
  • Works on Windows, Mac and Linux, using your computer's own keychain. No account, no subscription.
  • One command, stuntkey setup, and one yes.
  • Your AI works with a stand-in. StuntKey adds the real key on the way out and hides it in the answer.

StuntKey lowers the risk. It does not remove it, it is beta, and it has not had an independent security review. What it does not protect.
